Gentle Design Philosophy

Our aesthetic post-surgery mask is engineered with a skin-first approach. Every material selection and design decision prioritizes minimizing irritation to healing skin. The inner layer uses specially treated polypropylene fibers with a smoother surface finish (Ra < 0.5μm) compared to standard medical masks, significantly reducing mechanical friction against delicate facial skin.

The manufacturing process excludes any chemical treatments, fragrances, or optical brighteners. The mask is produced in a controlled environment with strict particulate and microbial controls, ensuring that each piece is clean and safe for immediate use on post-operative skin.

Dermatological Testing

The mask has undergone comprehensive dermatological testing including: Primary Skin Irritation Test (ISO 10993-10), Skin Sensitization Test (ISO 10993-10), and Cytotoxicity Test (ISO 10993-5). All tests confirmed zero irritation potential, zero sensitization risk, and non-cytotoxic properties.

Applications & Use Scenarios

This aesthetic post-surgery mask is specifically designed for post-operative care and sensitive skin applications:

Plastic & Cosmetic Surgery

Post-operative protection after facial procedures including rhinoplasty, blepharoplasty, and facelifts.

Dermatological Procedures

Post-treatment care after laser therapy, chemical peels, microdermabrasion, and injectable treatments.

Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery

Protection after dental implant surgery, wisdom tooth extraction, and jaw procedures.

Cardiac & Thoracic Surgery

Gentle protection for patients with compromised skin integrity after major surgeries.

Sensitive Skin Users

Ideal for individuals with eczema, rosacea, or other skin conditions requiring gentle contact materials.

Recovery & Rehabilitation

Extended wear during hospital recovery when skin sensitivity is heightened.
