About H-Guardian

Your Trusted Professional Protective Mask Manufacturer

Who We Are

H-Guardian (Nanjing Freeman Health Technology Co., Ltd.) is a leading global supplier of high-quality disposable protective masks, backed by our manufacturing base "Hubei Jialian Protection Products Co., Ltd." located in Xiantao, Hubei — the "Capital of Non-woven Products in China" with 23 years of industry experience.

We focus on R&D, production and sales of premium protective masks, with a core presence in the Japanese market and a growing global customer base across Southeast Asia, Middle East and Europe. Our products are widely used in daily protection, industrial safety and medical scenarios.
