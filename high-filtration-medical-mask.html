Filtration Mechanism

Our high filtration medical mask employs a dual-mechanism approach: mechanical blocking and electrostatic adsorption. The outer hydrophobic layer blocks large droplets and particles (>10μm). The dual meltblown layers use electret-treated polypropylene fibers that carry a permanent electrostatic charge, effectively capturing sub-micron particles (0.1-1.0μm) through electrostatic attraction — significantly more efficient than pure mechanical filtration alone.

The BFE test (ASTM F2101) uses Staphylococcus aureus aerosol (3.0μm) to verify bacterial filtration. The PFE test (ASTM F2299) uses 0.1μm latex particles to validate sub-micron particle capture. The VFE test uses bacteriophage ΦX174 (0.027μm) to confirm viral aerosol protection. All three metrics achieving ≥99% demonstrates comprehensive barrier performance.

Quality Control

Every production batch undergoes rigorous testing including: BFE/PFE/VFE verification, differential pressure measurement, fluid resistance testing, and microbial cleanliness assessment. Test reports from SGS and independent laboratories are available for each batch.
